# Bulgarian language

## Genetic and structural classification

Indo-European family, Slavic branch, South Slavic subgroup (Eastern group). Together with Macedonian, it stands out by having lost case declension and developed a postposed definite article.

## Number of users and geographic distribution

About 8 million speakers, mainly in Bulgaria. Minorities in Moldova, Ukraine, Serbia, Romania and Turkey.

## Dialects

East–west divide along the *yat* line (ять): eastern speakers pronounce "я" (e.g. *bjal*), western speakers "e" (*bel*).

## Phonetics

Six vowels (including ъ, a central vowel). Reduction of unstressed vowels. Rich system of hard and soft consonants.

## Writing system

Cyrillic script (30 letters). Bulgaria was the first to adopt Cyrillic in the 9th century, after the work of Cyril and Methodius.

## History

Old Church Slavonic (9th-11th c.) — the first Slavic literary language — derives from South Slavic dialects. Middle Bulgarian (12th-14th c.), Modern Bulgarian from the 19th century after the National Revival.
