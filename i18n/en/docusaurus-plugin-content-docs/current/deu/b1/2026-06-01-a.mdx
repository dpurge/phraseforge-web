# Lesson 1: Making Plans

```lesson-vocabulary lang=deu script=latn
der Plan {N m sg} [dehr plahn] = plan
lernen {V} [LAIR-nen] = to study
morgen {Adv} [MOR-gen] = tomorrow
um sechs Uhr {Time} [oom zeks oor] = at six o'clock
Passt dir das? {Question} [past deer das] = does that suit you?
vielleicht lieber {Adv} [fee-LAIKHT LEE-ber] = perhaps rather
```

```lesson-models lang=deu script=latn
Wir können morgen lernen [veer KÖN-en MOR-gen LAIR-nen] = We can study tomorrow
Passt dir sechs Uhr? [past deer zeks oor] = Does six o'clock suit you?
Vielleicht lieber am Freitag [fee-LAIKHT LEE-ber am FRAI-tag] = Perhaps Friday instead
```

```lesson-text lang=deu script=latn
**Einen Lernplan machen**

Wir können morgen zusammen lernen. Ich habe am Nachmittag Zeit.

Passt dir sechs Uhr? Wenn das zu spät ist, können wir früher anfangen.

Vielleicht lieber am Freitag. Dann haben wir mehr Zeit und können die neuen Wörter wiederholen.
```

```lesson-transcription lang=deu script=latn system="simple pronunciation"
Wir können morgen zusammen lernen. [veer KÖN-en MOR-gen tsoo-ZAH-men LAIR-nen]

Passt dir sechs Uhr? [past deer zeks oor]

Vielleicht lieber am Freitag. [fee-LAIKHT LEE-ber am FRAI-tag]
```

```lesson-translation lang=eng script=latn
**Making a Study Plan**

We can study together tomorrow. I have time in the afternoon.

Does six o'clock suit you? If that is too late, we can start earlier.

Perhaps Friday instead. Then we have more time and can review the new words.
```

```lesson-exercise lang=deu script=latn
Complete the German sentences.

1.  Wir können morgen _________.
2.  Passt dir _________ Uhr?
3.  Vielleicht lieber am _________.
```

```lesson-exercise lang=deu script=latn
Write a short dialogue.

2.  Ask about the time.

```
