# German

German is a major language of Central Europe and an important language of
science, culture, and business across the German-speaking region.

## Genetic Classification

German belongs to the Indo-European family, the Germanic branch, and the West
Germanic group. Standard German is historically based on High German varieties,
which are distinguished from Low German dialects partly by the effects of the
High German consonant shift.

## Speakers and Distribution

Around 130 million people worldwide speak German as a first or second language,
and the Goethe-Institut reports 15.5 million people learning German as a foreign
language. German is an official language in Germany, Austria, Liechtenstein,
Luxembourg, Belgium, and Switzerland, with German-speaking communities elsewhere
in Europe and beyond.

## History

The history of German is commonly divided into Old High German, Middle High
German, and Early New High German. The written standard developed through
printing, religious texts, and administrative use, with the Reformation playing
an important role in wider written circulation. In the 19th and 20th centuries,
Standard German became firmly established in education, administration,
literature, and mass media.
