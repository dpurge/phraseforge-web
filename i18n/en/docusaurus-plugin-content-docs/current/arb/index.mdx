# Arabic language

## Genetic and structural classification

Afro-Asiatic family, Semitic branch, Central Semitic subgroup. An inflected language with a system of triconsonantal roots.

## Number of users and geographic distribution

About 380 million native speakers. Official language in 22 countries of the Middle East and North Africa. Liturgical language of Islam.

## Dialects

Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) used in media and writing. Major spoken dialects: Egyptian, Levantine, Maghrebi, Gulf, Iraqi, Sudanese.

## Phonetics

Characteristic pharyngeal and emphatic consonants (ص ض ط ظ ق ع ح). Three short and three long vowels.

## Writing system

Arabic alphabet, written from right to left. 28 letters, with shapes depending on their position in the word. Short vowels are optionally written with diacritics.

## History

Descended from the older Arabic dialects of the Arabian Peninsula. Standardized in the 7th century AD with the text of the Quran. Classical Arabic became the language of science and administration across the Muslim world.
