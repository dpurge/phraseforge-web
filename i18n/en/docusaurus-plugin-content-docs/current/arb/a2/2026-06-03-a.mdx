<Text lang="arb" script="arb">

## Why did the Euphrates River rise in Syria?

Fayez Abbas, chairman of the emergency committee in Deir ez-Zour, said that the region exceeded the stage of danger after the rise of the Euphrates River waters.

The Euphrates River noted the rise in its water level. This happened because of floods in north and east Syria. The cause of the floods is heavy rains. Also, more waters came from Turkey.

The Syrian Ministry of Energy explained that the cause is heavy rainfall this year, as well as opening the dams in Turkey.

But this flood came after years of drought and water shortage in the Euphrates in Syria. So how did this happen?

</Text>

<Transcription lang="arb" script="arb" system="DIN 31635">

## Limaḏā fāḍa nahru al-Furāt fī Sūriyā?

Fāyiz ʿAbbās, raʾīsu laǧnati aṭ-Ṭawāriʾ fī Dair az-Zūr, qāla inna al-minṭaqata taǧāwazat marḥalata al-ḫaṭari baʿda irtifāʿi miyāhi nahri al-Furāt.

Nahru al-Furāt šahida irtifāʿan fī mansūbi al-miyāh. Hāḏā ḥadaṯa bisababi faiḍānātin fī šimāli wa-šarqi Sūriyā. Sababu al-faiḍānāti huwa al-amṭāru al-kaṯīra. Wa-aiḍan, ǧāʾat miyāhun akṯara min Turkiyā.

Wizāratu aṭ-Ṭāqati as-Sūriyya awḍaḥat anna as-sababa huwa amṭārun ġazīratun hāḏihi as-sanah, wa-kaḏālika fatḥu bawwābāti as-sudūdi fī Turkiyā.

Lakin hāḏā al-faiḍānu atā baʿda sanawātin mina al-ǧafāfi wa-naqṣi al-miyāhi fī al-Furāt bi-Sūriyā. Fa-kaifa ḥadaṯa ḏālika?

</Transcription>

## Why did the Euphrates River rise in Syria?

Fayez Abbas, chairman of the emergency committee in Dajr az-Zaur, said that the region passed the stage of danger after the rise of the water levels in the Euphrates River.

The Euphrates River noted the rise in its water levels. This happened because of floods in north and east Syria. The cause of the floods is heavy rains. Furthermore, more waters came from Turkey.

The Syrian Ministry of Energy explained that the cause is heavy rainfall this year, as well as opening the dams in Turkey.

But this flood came after years of drought and water shortage in the Euphrates in Syria. So how did this happen?
