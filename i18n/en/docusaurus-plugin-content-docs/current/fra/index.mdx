# French language

## Genetic and structural classification

Indo-European family, Romance (Italic) branch, Gallo-Romance subgroup. Inflected in the verb, isolating in nominal morphology.

## Number of users and geographic distribution

About 80 million native speakers, 270-320 million in total. France, Belgium, Switzerland, Luxembourg, Monaco, Canada (Quebec), sub-Saharan Africa (Senegal, Mali, Ivory Coast, DRC), the Maghreb.

## Dialects

The literary standard is based on the Parisian dialect. Other varieties: *langue d'oïl* (north, including Picard, Norman, Walloon), *langue d'oc* (Occitan — often considered a separate language), Arpitan (Franco-Provençal), French-based creoles (Haitian, Mauritian).

## Phonetics

Nasal vowels (*an, en, in, on, un*), no word stress (rhythmic group stress instead). *Liaison* — linking final consonants to a following vowel. Uvular *r*.

## Writing system

Latin alphabet with diacritics (*é è ê ë à â ï î ô ù û ç*). Highly etymological spelling — a wide gap between writing and pronunciation.

## History

Descended from Vulgar (Gallo-Romance) Latin. Old French (9th-14th c.) — *Strasbourg Oaths*, 842. Middle French (14th-16th c.). Standardized by the Edict of Villers-Cotterêts (1539) and the Académie française (1635).
