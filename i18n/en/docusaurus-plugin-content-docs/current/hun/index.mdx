# Hungarian language

## Genetic and structural classification

Uralic family, Finno-Ugric branch, Ugric subgroup (together with Khanty and Mansi). An agglutinative language — 18 basic cases, no grammatical gender.

## Number of users and geographic distribution

About 13 million speakers. Hungary, along with significant minorities in Romania (Transylvania), Slovakia, Serbia (Vojvodina), Ukraine (Transcarpathia), and Austria (Burgenland).

## Dialects

Relatively uniform. Main groups: Western, Danubian, *Palóc*, Northeastern, Mezőség, Székely (Transylvanian), and Csángó (Moldavia, the most archaic).

## Phonetics

Fourteen vowels with vowel harmony (front/back, rounded/unrounded). Long and short vowels and consonants. Stress is always on the first syllable.

## Writing system

Latin alphabet with diacritics (*á é í ó ö ő ú ü ű*) and digraphs (*cs, sz, zs, gy, ly, ny, ty*). Earlier (until the 13th century), the runic script *rovásírás* was used.

## History

The Magyars arrived in the Pannonian Basin in the late 9th century. The oldest surviving Hungarian text is *Halotti beszéd* ("Funeral Sermon"), ca. 1192-1195. The language reform of the late 18th / early 19th century (Ferenc Kazinczy) modernized the vocabulary.
