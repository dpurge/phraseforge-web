# The garden

```vocabulary lang=lat script=latn
hortus, -i {m} = garden
amicus, -i {m} = friend
pila, -ae {f} = ball
meus, -a, -um = my
tuus, -a, -um = your (sg.)
gratus, -a, -um = welcome, pleasing
molestus, -a, -um = troublesome, annoying
invitare = to invite
obtemperare = to obey, to comply
exspectare = to wait for, to await
delectare = to delight, to please
properare = to hurry, to hasten
sum {V} = I am (esse = to be)
ego {Pron} = I
tu {Pron} = you (sg.)
nos {Pron} = we
vos {Pron} = you (pl.)
me {Pron acc} = me
te {Pron acc} = you (sg., acc.)
in {Prep} = in, into
at {Conj} = but, however
libenter {Adv} = gladly, willingly
magnopere {Adv} = greatly, very much
non {Adv} = not
```

```models lang=lat script=latn
hortus = garden
hortus meus = my garden
in hortum meum = into my garden
In hortum meum invito te, Tite! = I invite you into my garden, Titus!

amici = friends
amici mei = my friends
In horti amici mei sunt. = My friends are in the garden.

libenter = gladly
obtempero = I comply
Libenter obtempero. = I gladly comply.
molestus non sum = I am not troublesome
Amicis tuis molestus non sum? = Am I not troublesome to your friends?

gratus es = you are welcome
At tu gratus es amicis. = But you are welcome to the friends.
Amici mei et me et te exspectant. = My friends are waiting for both me and you.

pila = ball
pila vos delectat = the ball delights you
Pila-ne vos delectat? = Does the ball delight you?
Magnopere nos pila delectat. = The ball delights us greatly.
Propera, amice! = Hurry, friend!
```
