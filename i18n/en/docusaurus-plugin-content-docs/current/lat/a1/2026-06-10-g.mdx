<Text as="translation" lang="eng" script="latn">

## The Roman

The Roman hurries to the forum.
The Roman's garment is a white toga.
Steadfastness adorns the Roman.
In the forum and in the curia the Romans deliberate about the homeland.
The wisdom and steadfastness of the Romans secure great glory for the homeland.

</Text>
