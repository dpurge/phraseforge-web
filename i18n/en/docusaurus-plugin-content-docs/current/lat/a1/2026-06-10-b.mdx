<Text lang="lat" script="latn">
Parvae puellae agna grata est.
Puella agnae cenam parat.
Domina puellis imperat:
"Agnis aquam portate, puellae!"
Puellae dominae obtemperant.
Bestiis aquam portant.
</Text>

<Text as="translation" lang="eng" script="latn">

## In the yard

The lamb is dear to the small girl.
The girl prepares a meal for the lamb.
The mistress commands the girls:
"Carry water to the lambs, girls!"
The girls obey the mistress.
They carry water to the animals.

</Text>
