<Text lang="lat" script="latn">
Quintus:
"Quam pulcher est liber tuus, Marce!"

Marcus:
"Liber magistri nostri donum est.
Boni pueri magistro nostro grati sunt.
Bonos pueros magister coronis et libris pulchris donat.
In libro meo multae et pulchrae fabulae sunt."

Quintus:
"Gratane tibi est schola vestra?"

Marcus:
"Schola nostra mihi grata est.
Magister, vir doctus, fabulis pulchris animos nostros delectat."
</Text>

<Text as="translation" lang="eng" script="latn">

## The teacher

Quintus:
"How beautiful your book is, Marcus!"

Marcus:
"The book is a gift from our teacher.
Good boys are grateful to our teacher.
The teacher presents the good boys with wreaths and beautiful books.
In my book there are many beautiful stories."

Quintus:
"Is your school pleasant to you?"

Marcus:
"Our school is pleasant to me.
The teacher, a learned man, delights our minds with beautiful stories."

</Text>
