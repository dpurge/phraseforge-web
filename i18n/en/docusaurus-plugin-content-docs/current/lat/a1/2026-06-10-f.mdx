<Text lang="lat" script="latn">
Templum dei magnificum est.
Templum columnae ornant.
Domicilia oppidi templum superat.
Multa dona sunt in templo.
Nam deus claro oppido propitius est.
Ad templum turba magna properat.

Cuncti vestimentis albis et coronis ornati sunt.
Magnam copiam donorum portant.
Sacrificiis et donis gratiam dei oppido patrio et domiciliis suis conciliant.
</Text>

<Text as="translation" lang="eng" script="latn">

## The temple

The temple of the god is magnificent.
Columns adorn the temple.
[The temple] surpasses the dwellings of the town.
There are many gifts in the temple.
For the god is favourable to the famous town.
A great crowd hurries to the temple.

All are adorned with white garments and wreaths.
They carry a great abundance of gifts.
With sacrifices and gifts they win the favour of the god for their native town and for their own homes.

</Text>
