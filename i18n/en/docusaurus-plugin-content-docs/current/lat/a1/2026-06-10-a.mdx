<Text lang="lat" script="latn">
Ranae aquam amant.
Magna rana parvam ranam vituperat:
"Ripam vita, filia!
Ripas ciconiae amant.
Saevas ciconias vita!"

Sapientiam magnae ranae filia vituperat:
"Ego herbas riparum amo.
Herbae parvas ranarum figuras occultant."
Poena stultitiae magna est.
Stultam ranam ciconia devorat.
</Text>

<Text as="translation" lang="eng" script="latn">

## The old frog and the young frog

Frogs love water.
The great frog scolds the small frog:
"Avoid the bank, daughter!
Storks love the banks.
Avoid the cruel storks!"

The daughter scolds the wisdom of the great frog:
"I love the grasses of the banks.
The grasses hide the small figures of the frogs."
The punishment for foolishness is great.
The stork devours the foolish frog.

</Text>
