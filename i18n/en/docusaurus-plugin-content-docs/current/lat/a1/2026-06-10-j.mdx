<Text lang="lat" script="latn">
Mamercus nauta Camilli agricolae fortunam laudat:
"Beatus es, agricola!
Quam iucunda est agricolarum vita!
Beati agricolae agros patrios arant, a periculis liberi sunt.
Etiam poetae clari fortunam vestram laudant, agricolae beati."

Tum Camillus:
"Ego tuam, nauta beate, fortunam laudo.
Nautae periti magnas sibi divitias parant.
Navigia nautis beatis frumentum, aurum, argentum portant."
</Text>

<Text as="translation" lang="eng" script="latn">

## The farmer Camillus and the sailor Mamercus

The sailor Mamercus praises the fortune of the farmer Camillus:
"You are happy, farmer!
How pleasant the life of farmers is!
Happy farmers plough their ancestral fields and are free from dangers.
Even famous poets praise your fortune, happy farmers."

Then Camillus:
"I praise your fortune, happy sailor.
Skilled sailors obtain great riches for themselves.
Ships carry grain, gold, and silver to happy sailors."

</Text>
