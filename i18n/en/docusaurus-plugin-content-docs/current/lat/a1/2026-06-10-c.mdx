<Text lang="lat" script="latn">
Silva ornata est statua Minervae deae.
Minerva galea armata est.
Puellae coronis deae aram ornant.

Ubi est statua Minervae?

Statua Minervae in silva est.

Quo properant puellae?

Puellae in silvam properant.
Coronas in ara collocant.
</Text>

<Text as="translation" lang="eng" script="latn">

## The goddess Minerva

The forest is adorned with a statue of the goddess Minerva.
Minerva is armed with a helmet.
The girls adorn the altar of the goddess with wreaths.

Where is the statue of Minerva?

The statue of Minerva is in the forest.

Where are the girls hurrying?

The girls hurry into the forest.
They place the wreaths on the altar.

</Text>
