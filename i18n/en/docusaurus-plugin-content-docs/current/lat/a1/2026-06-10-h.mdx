<Text as="translation" lang="eng" script="latn">

## Marcus, a Roman boy

Marcus hurries to school.
Marcus is a Roman boy.
Many boys come running.
The Roman boy's garment is a toga.
The tunics of the Roman boys are also garments.
The slaves carry tablets for the boys.
The fate of the slaves is wretched.
But faithful slaves are dear to free men.

</Text>
