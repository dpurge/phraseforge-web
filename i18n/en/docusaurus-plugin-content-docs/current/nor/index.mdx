# Norwegian language

## Genetic and structural classification

Indo-European family, Germanic branch, North Germanic subgroup (West Scandinavian). Mutually intelligible with Swedish and Danish.

## Number of users and geographic distribution

About 5.3 million speakers. Norway. Minorities in the USA and Canada (diaspora).

## Dialects

Two officially recognized written standards: *Bokmål* (based on Danish, used by the majority) and *Nynorsk* (built in the 19th century by Ivar Aasen from the western dialects). There is no spoken standard — dialects are used in speech.

## Phonetics

Pitch (musical) accent — distinguishes word pairs (*bønder* / *bønner*). Long and short vowels. The consonant *kj* (a palatal fricative).

## Writing system

Latin alphabet with the letters *æ ø å* (the letter *å* replaced the older *aa* in 1917).

## History

From Old Norse (11th-14th c.). Under Danish rule (1380-1814) Danish was the written language, which shaped *Bokmål*. In the 19th century — national revival and the creation of *Landsmål* (from 1929 *Nynorsk*). The coexistence of the two forms is regulated by successive laws.
