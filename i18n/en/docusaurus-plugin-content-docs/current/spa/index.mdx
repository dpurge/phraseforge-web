# Spanish

Spanish, also known as Castilian, is one of the world's major Romance
languages. This page gives a short language background before the PhraseForge
lessons.

## Genetic Classification

Spanish belongs to the Indo-European family, the Italic branch, and the Romance
group. It developed from Vulgar Latin on the Iberian Peninsula and is usually
classified within Western Romance and Ibero-Romance.

## Speakers and Distribution

Instituto Cervantes reported that Spanish passed 600 million potential users
worldwide in 2024, including almost 500 million native speakers. The language is
used in Spain, most of Latin America, Equatorial Guinea, and large
Spanish-speaking communities in the United States.

## History

Spanish developed from local varieties of Latin after the Romanization of the
Iberian Peninsula. In the Middle Ages, the Castilian variety became especially
important and later formed the basis of the written standard. From the late 15th
century onward, political consolidation in Spain and colonization in the
Americas spread the language beyond Europe and shaped many regional varieties.
