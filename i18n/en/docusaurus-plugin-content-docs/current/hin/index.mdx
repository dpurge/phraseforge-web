# Hindi language

## Genetic and structural classification

Indo-European family, Indo-Iranian branch, Indo-Aryan (Central) subgroup. Together with Urdu it forms *Hindustani* — a shared spoken language, distinguished mainly by script and by the source of formal vocabulary (Sanskritic vs. Perso-Arabic).

## Number of users and geographic distribution

About 340 million native speakers, around 600 million in total. India (an official language alongside English), Fiji, Mauritius, Suriname, and diaspora communities in the USA, UK, and the Gulf.

## Dialects

Standard Hindi is based on *Khari Boli* in the Delhi region. Eastern and Western varieties are sometimes treated as separate languages: *Braj*, *Awadhi*, *Bhojpuri*, *Rajasthani*, *Haryanvi*.

## Phonetics

Four-way distinction of stop consonants: voiceless/voiced × unaspirated/aspirated. Retroflex consonants (*ṭ ḍ ṇ*). Nasal vowels.

## Writing system

Devanagari — an abugida, written left to right, with the characteristic horizontal line (*shirorekha*) connecting the letters.

## History

Descended from Sanskrit through Prakrits and Middle Indo-Aryan languages (*Apabhraṃśa*). *Khari Boli* under the Delhi Sultanate (13th-16th c.). Standardization of Hindi in the 19th century — distinguished from Urdu during the colonial period.
