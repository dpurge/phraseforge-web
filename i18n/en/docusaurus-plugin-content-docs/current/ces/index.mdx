# Czech language

## Genetic and structural classification

Indo-European family, Slavic branch, West Slavic subgroup (Czech-Slovak group). An inflected language with seven cases.

## Number of users and geographic distribution

About 10.5 million speakers. Czech Republic, with minorities in Slovakia, Austria, USA and Canada.

## Dialects

Czech dialects (central and the common spoken interdialect — *obecná čeština*), Moravian, and Lach-Silesian. The literary standard is historically conservative compared with living speech.

## Phonetics

Distinctive ř (a raised alveolar trill, a unique phoneme). Long and short vowels are phonemically distinct. Stress is always on the first syllable.

## Writing system

Latin alphabet with diacritics — háček (š č ž ř) and the length mark (á é í ó ú). The system was proposed by Jan Hus in the early 15th century.

## History

From Old Czech (12th-15th c.) — *Dalimil's Chronicle*, Hus. Literary flowering in the 16th century (*Kralice Bible*). National Revival in the 19th century (Jungmann, Dobrovský).
